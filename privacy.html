Privacy Policy — Workflow Exporter Bridge

Last updated: October 4, 2026

Workflow Exporter Bridge is a private tool used only by its owner's family.

  • What it accesses: read-only access to the owner's own Gmail, and the ability to create events on the owner's own Google Calendar.
  • How data is used: only to identify school and activity updates and add them to the owner's calendar.
  • Where data is stored: on the owner's personal computer. It is never sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
  • Revoking access: remove the app at any time from your Google Account permissions.

Google API data use complies with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
